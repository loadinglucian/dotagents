# Phrase Patterns

## Permission and capability

- `You may...`
- `You can also...`
- `You are free to...`
- `...allows you to easily...`

## Defaults and conventions

- `By default...`
- `By convention...`
- `Unless otherwise specified...`
- `Typically...`

## Guidance and reassurance

- `It's important to understand...`
- `Remember...`
- `Don't worry...`
- `Keep in mind...`

## Technical explanation bridges

- `...can be thought of as...`
- `...meaning...`
- `...allowing your application to...`

## Avoid

- `just`
- `simply`
- passive constructions like `The file should be created`

Rewrite as imperative active voice:
- `Create the file...`
